# Business Requirments

## Objective

Build an end-to-end BI platform for analyzing global retail sales, customers,products,geography,profitability
and shipping performance.

## Users

- Executive Management
- Sales Management
- Regional Managers
- Product Managers
- Operations Managers

## Business Questions

1. What are total sales and profits? 
2. Which products perform best?
3. Which customers generate the most revenue?
4. Which regionsd are most profitable?
5. Which markets perform best?
6. How does dicount affect profitability?
7. Which shipping modes are most used?
8. How long does shipping take?
9. How are sales changing over time?

## KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin
- Average Discount
- Average Shipping Time
- Sales Growth
